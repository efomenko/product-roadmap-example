# Product Strategy

## Purpose

Explains the strategic choices required to achieve the product vision. It describes where the product will focus, what customer problems it will prioritize, and how the product will create differentiated customer and business value.

## Strategic Pillar 1 — Simplicity

Reduce the effort required to create and understand workflows.

## Strategic Pillar 2 — Reliability

Make automation predictable, observable, and recoverable.

## Strategic Pillar 3 — Extensibility

Allow the platform to support new integrations and use cases.

## Strategic Pillar 4 — Scale

Support growing numbers of customers, workflows, and executions.

## Strategic Pillar 5 — Intelligence

Use AI where it provides meaningful value without compromising
control and reliability.
