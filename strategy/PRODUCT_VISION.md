# Product Vision

## Purpose

Defines the long-term customer outcome the product is intended to achieve. The vision provides direction for future product decisions without prescribing specific features or implementation details.

## Vision

Enable IT teams to automate operational processes easily, reliably,
and at scale.

## Product Promise

Users should be able to move from:

"I have a repetitive operational problem"

to:

"I have a reliable automation handling this problem"

without requiring custom development.

## Long-Term Vision

The platform evolves from a workflow configuration tool into an intelligent
automation platform combining:

- Deterministic workflows
- Integrations
- Reusable templates
- Analytics
- AI-assisted workflow creation
- AI-powered recommendations
